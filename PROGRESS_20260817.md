# Progress 2026-08-17

## Objective

Prepare dedicated high-frequency command trajectories for stereo measurement
of the PAT command-to-particle response, especially around the unexplained
70--110 Hz band, as the next step toward open-loop control.

## Implemented

- Added `Acoutools_eventcam/hf_identification_trajectory.py`.
- Added `Acoutools_eventcam/high_frequency_identification_plan.json`.
- Added displacement, velocity, and acceleration safety checks with automatic
  whole-command scaling.
- Added a hardware loader that applies the levitation centre and supports an
  initial 50% command-scale trial with a second safety check.
- Added NPZ, offset CSV, metadata JSON, and trajectory preview export.
- Kept delay feedforward disabled for identification so the measured input is
  the unmodified PAT command.
- Added `Acoutools_eventcam/HIGH_FREQUENCY_IDENTIFICATION_HANDOFF.md` for the
  separate measurement PC.

## Measurement plan

1. Two static baseline recordings.
2. One x-axis 40--130 Hz chirp at 50% command scale.
3. Full configured x/y chirp recordings after checking particle retention and
   event visibility.
4. x/y 50--130 Hz multisine recordings with multiple phase seeds.
5. Keep the z-axis 220--350 Hz diagnostic disabled until the stereo noise floor
   and particle retention have been confirmed.

## Numerical validation

- Chirp maximum: approximately 0.120 mm, 48.95 mm/s, and 19,989 mm/s^2.
- Multisine runs are automatically scaled to stay at or below 28,000 mm/s^2.
- Sixteen unit tests, including the existing model and delay tests, passed.
- Python compilation and Git whitespace checks passed.

## Measurement-PC integration completed

- The measurement-only `acoustools_stereo_eventcam_3d_recording_auto.py` and
  `stereo_acoustools_3d_recording_core.py` were found and integrated with
  `load_trajectory_for_hardware` without changing camera synchronization,
  PAT-start LED timing, serials, ROI, or calibration defaults.
- `hf_identification_trajectory.py` and
  `high_frequency_identification_plan.json` were installed at the workspace
  root and 12 enabled commands were exported to `hf_identification_export/`.
- The returned absolute positions use the existing `ideal_log.csv` writer.
  Exact source command artifacts and hashes are stored with every HF run.
- Dynamic commands above 50% scale require the explicit
  `--acknowledge-hf-retention-and-visibility` gate.
- Full-scale and 50% x-chirp dry-runs completed without opening hardware. All
  12 enabled previews were inspected, Python compilation passed, and 20
  targeted `unittest` tests passed.

## Next measurement action

The two static baselines and initial 50% x-axis chirp were recorded. Particle
visibility, retention, hardware synchronization, and LED detection were good,
but the old camera margin omitted the final approximately 0.197 s of the chirp.
